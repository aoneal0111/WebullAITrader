# Historical knowledge in Warrior

The PAPER desktop composition loads the historical decision-intelligence
artifact as a read-only evidence source. Its runtime lookup now preserves
whole-sample and TEST 2% target rates, chronological split counts and descriptive
policy stability. Sample-size labels such as LARGE_SAMPLE are preserved instead
of being collapsed to INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE. They are not execution confidence.

DI-ENTRY reports RESEARCH_SUPPORTED when a descriptive LARGE_SAMPLE or MODERATE
row has at least 30 observations in each chronological split and a recorded
STABLE policy status. This is an explicit descriptive coverage screen, not a
statistical independence test or profitability criterion. Overlapping episodes
can inflate apparent sample counts. No walk-forward result is fabricated from
policy stability. Such evidence remains blocked from entry treatment with
RESEARCH_ONLY_NOT_EXECUTION_VALIDATED. Existing explicitly validated confidence
labels keep their prior treatment behavior.

All 23 ACTIVE taxonomy strategies are already included in the default PAPER
execution adapter. Tests verify every one passes the normalized contract and
projects its strategy identity, trigger and stop into Warrior. This does not
mean every detector fires, every opportunity is tradable, or every strategy has
historical samples. GAP_AND_GO_CONTINUATION and POST_GAP_RECLAIM currently have
zero samples in the user's artifact. Feature availability, confirmed geometry,
freshness and risk authorization still apply.

The taxonomy bridge currently supplies neutral membership scores. Historical
research does not yet rank actual competing entries, change sizing or select
exit policies. The default historical entry experiment remains disabled and
OBSERVE_ONLY. This change does not enable it, change the Scalper, or create orders.

After installing while Atlas is stopped, restart normally and inspect newly
dated runtime observations. A running observer caches artifact rows, so replacing
the database alone cannot prove that a previously launched process reloaded it.
Check setup_evidence for train_sample_count, validation_sample_count,
test_sample_count, target_2_rate, test_target_2_rate and policy_stability.
Check the new observation timestamp against the new runtime start. Old journal
records remain historical; they are not rewritten by this change.
